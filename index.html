Questions

Will you tell me a site is a scam?

No. The result is “looks ordinary,” “review these signals,” or “unavailable.” A signal is a reason to slow down, not a verdict.

Do you scan email and text messages?

Paste a link and the checker looks at that public address. It does not log into mailboxes.

Can this be used to attack a site?

No. Private addresses are blocked, bodies are capped, and the report returns signals rather than the page source.
